SIMPLE 4-STEP ONBOARDING

Membership Eligibility & Required Documents

We maintain an open-door, non-discriminatory enrollment policy designed to support marginal, tenant, and women farmers across our operational districts.

Who Can Become a Member?

  • Active smallholder, marginal, or medium land-owning farmers.
  • Tenant farmers and oral lessees with local village panchayat cultivation certification.
  • Women farmer self-help groups (SHGs) and Joint Liability Groups (JLGs).
  • Commitment to adhere to fair harvesting, moisture standards, and non-chemical practices.

Simple Documentation Required

  • Government photo ID (Aadhaar Card or Voter ID).
  • Land revenue passbook (Patta/Chitta) OR notarized lease agreement.
  • Cancelled cheque leaf or bank passbook copy (for DBT bank transfers).
  • Two recent passport-size photographs.

Membership share capital is ₹500 (one-time equity contribution) granting full lifelong shareholder voting rights.

MUTUAL ACCOUNTABILITY

Member Responsibilities & Code of Farming Integrity

Collective prosperity depends on reciprocal trust. As an equal shareholder of GreenRoots Producer Company, each grower commits to fair operational practices.

Pesticide Withdrawal

Adhering strictly to mandatory 15-day pre-harvest synthetic pesticide withdrawal periods to maintain zero-residue certification.

Clean Field Drying

Drying harvested paddy and millets on clean tarpaulins to eliminate gravel, foreign seeds, and moisture contamination before collection.

Democratic Participation

Attending Annual General Meetings (AGMs) and monthly village cluster assemblies to review audited financial accounts and vote on company policies.

Direct Batch Delivery

Directly delivering produce to authorized village collection kiosks without routing through unverified local middlemen or commercial aggregators.
